0.9.0

  • Check common sources of disk use, including developer storage and personal files you can review.
  • Browse nested folders beside a cleanup assessment. Review which items will move to Trash and restore eligible items from the cleanup receipt.
  • Open Projects, Docker, Devices, and Local models from the Developer tabs. Simulator devices have their own view.
  • See when Trash requires macOS permission and retry the scan after resolving access.
  • Optionally ask an AI provider to explain ambiguous files. Preview the information shared before sending from Browse and cleanup review.

The app preserves existing scan history and receipts when upgrading from 0.8.x. macOS may ask for access again.
